Elphaba & Glinda

Elphaba Thropp is a misunderstood prodigy who seeks justice for the sentient animals of Oz. Despite facing relentless prejudice, her convictions remain unshakable.

Glinda Upland begins as an image-obsessed socialite who ultimately learns the emotional burden of leadership and moral responsibility.

Key Themes

Perception vs. Reality: How public perception shapes truth.

Friendship: A bond that alters two lives for good.
