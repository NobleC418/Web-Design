The 8 Core Requirements for Off-Grid Living

Living off the grid means becoming self-reliant and disconnecting from public utilities. Based on Off Grid World's guide, here are the eight main things you need in place:

  1. Land: Find property with good soil, sunlight, water access, and local zoning laws that allow off-grid homes.
  2. Shelter: Build a sturdy, well-insulated home like a cabin, earthship, or tiny house suited for the local climate.
  3. Water: Secure a reliable source such as a well, spring, creek, or rainwater collection and filtration system.
  4. Food: Grow a garden, raise livestock, preserve harvests, and store long-term dry goods.
  5. Energy: Generate your own electricity with solar panels, wind turbines, or micro-hydro systems paired with battery banks.
  6. Waste Management: Use composting toilets, greywater recycling, and septic systems to handle waste safely.
  7. Security: Protect your homestead with fencing, lighting, dogs, and strong locks.
  8. Community: Connect with neighbors and local homesteaders to trade skills, tools, and support.
