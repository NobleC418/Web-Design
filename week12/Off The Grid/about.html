What Does Off-Grid Mean?

Going off-grid is about reducing dependence on municipal power, city water, and commercial supply chains. Many people choose this lifestyle to lower monthly bills, live closer to nature, and gain independence.

Planning ahead is critical. Before buying land or building a shelter, beginners should test water quality, calculate daily solar wattage needs, and learn basic food preservation and gardening skills.
