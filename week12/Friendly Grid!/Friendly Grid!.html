Why It Stands Out

Brooklyn Nine-Nine follows Detective Jake Peralta, a talented yet carefree investigator in Brooklyn's fictional 99th Precinct, whose dynamic shifts when the stern and by-the-book Captain Raymond Holt takes command.

Rather than relying on mean-spirited humor, the series builds comedy through sharp cold opens, running gags like the annual Halloween Heist, and genuine camaraderie across its ensemble cast. Each character brings a distinct comedic rhythm—from Amy Santiago's binder-obsessed perfectionism to Rosa Diaz's deadpan stoicism and Terry Jeffords' protective energy.

Over eight seasons, the show balances fast-paced absurdism with heartfelt character growth, making it endlessly rewatchable.

Quick Facts

  • Aired: 2013 – 2021
  • Seasons: 8 (153 Episodes)
  • Creators: Dan Goor & Michael Schur
  • Best Episode Arc: Annual Halloween Heists
